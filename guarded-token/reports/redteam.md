# Red-team drill: attacks vs the Phase 4 guardian

Each attack ran on a fresh token after ~3 simulated days of normal trading by 20 users and an exchange, with normal trading continuing during the attack. Generated by `ape run redteam`.

| Attack | Mode | Escaped (GTT) | Frozen (GTT) | Victim kept (GTT) | Fake volume (GTT) | Attack transfers that went through | Innocent users blocklisted | Normal transfers failed | Token paused |

Why normal transfers failed: hourly limit exceeded (8). The guardian's limit tightening applies to everyone, so busy wallets such as the exchange hit the lower hourly limit until the admin raises it again.

**Escaped** = cashed out at the exchange + left in attacker wallets that are not blocklisted. **Frozen** = stuck in blocklisted attacker wallets. The victim's own wallet is blocklisted too when it is part of an incident (its key is presumed stolen); the admin unblocks it after the owner secures a new key.

**Limits:** one run per attack with a fixed random seed, on a small simulated population; the models were trained on the Phase 3 simulator, not on these exact wallets. Treat the numbers as a drill, not a guarantee.
